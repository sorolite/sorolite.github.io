Vector Basics

Practice problems for vector basics.

Problem 1

Let P=(−2,1)P=(-2,1) and Q=(4,9)Q=(4,9). Find PQ→\overrightarrow{PQ}.

Problem 2

Let Q=(3,−4)Q=(3,-4) and R=(−2,8)R=(-2,8). Find QR→\overrightarrow{QR}.

Problem 3

Let A=(5,2)A=(5,2) and B=(−3,−4)B=(-3,-4). Find AB→\overrightarrow{AB}.

Problem 4

Find PQ→\overrightarrow{PQ} and |PQ||PQ| for P=(1,−2,3)P=(1,-2,3) and Q=(5,1,−9)Q=(5,1,-9).

Problem 5

Find ST→\overrightarrow{ST} and |ST||ST| for S=(−3,4,1)S=(-3,4,1) and T=(3,−2,4)T=(3,-2,4).

Problem 6

Find CD→\overrightarrow{CD} and |CD||CD| for C=(2,1,−1)C=(2,1,-1) and D=(−2,3,3)D=(-2,3,3).

Problem 7

Find the unit vector in the direction of ⟨5,12⟩\langle5,12\rangle; express it using 𝐢,𝐣\mathbf i,\mathbf j.

Problem 8

Find the unit vector in the direction of −8𝐢+6𝐣-8\mathbf{i} + 6\mathbf{j}. Express it using angle brackets ⟨,⟩\langle\ ,\ \rangle.

Problem 9

Find the unit vector in the opposite direction of ⟨2,−3,6⟩\langle2,-3,6\rangle. Express it using 𝐢,𝐣,𝐤\mathbf i,\mathbf j,\mathbf k.

Problem 10

Find the vector of magnitude 77 in the direction of ⟨3,−4⟩\langle3,-4\rangle.

Problem 11

Find the vector of magnitude 1010 at an angle π/6\pi/6 counterclockwise from the positive xx-axis.

Problem 12

Find the vector of magnitude 88 making angle 3π/43\pi/4 clockwise from the positive xx-axis.

Problem 13

Find the distances from (3,−4,5)(3,-4,5) to the xyxy, xzxz, and yzyz planes.

Problem 14

On a street grid with square blocks of the same size, a walker travels 1212 blocks east then 1515 blocks north. Letting east be the positive xx direction and north be the positive yy direction, represent this motion as a sum of two (nonzero) orthogonal vectors. Find the magnitude of the total displacement vector.

Problem 15

A boat is travelling in a large river, with velocity relative to the water of 10m/s10\,\mathrm{m/s} due north. The river is flowing 25m/s25\,\mathrm{m/s} due east. Find the velocity and the speed of the boat relative to the shore.

Problem 16

A campsite is placed at coordinates labelled (2,−5)(2, -5) on a map, where the units of distance are kilometres. A lookout on the same map has coordinates (−5,7)(-5, 7). Give a unit vector pointing from the campsite towards the lookout. A camper walks 3 km directly from the camp in a straight line in that direction; give the displacement vector of her walk.

Problem 17

Two cables pull a ring: one exerts 22N22\,\mathrm N at 30∘30^\circ above the positive xx-axis, and the other exerts 7N7\,\mathrm N at 120∘120^\circ above the positive xx-axis. Find the resultant force vector. Also find the magnitude of this force.

Problem 18

A drone is 12m12\,\mathrm m east, 5m5\,\mathrm m north, and 9m9\,\mathrm m above its launch point. Use these directions as positive x,y,zx,y,z. Find its position vector. Also find the straight-line distance of the drone from its launch point.

Problem 19

A sensor at coordinate position (4,−2,3)(4,-2,3) (with coordinates measured in metres) detects objects 6m6\,\mathrm m or less away. Can it detect an object at the coordinates (4,4,3)(4,4,3)? At (1,0,1)? At (5,5,2)?
