Partial derivatives

Local change in functions of several variables.

Problem 1

Find the gradient of f(x,y)=x2y+sin(xy) at (1,0).

Reveal solution
fx=2xy+ycos(xy),fy=x2+xcos(xy)

At (1,0), these are 0 and 2. Therefore ∇f(1,0)=⟨0,2⟩.

Problem 2

Find the tangent plane to z=x2+2y2 at (1,1,3).

Reveal solution

Since fx=2x and fy=4y, the slopes at (1,1) are 2 and 4.

z−3=2(x−1)+4(y−1)

Equivalently, z=2x+4y−3.

Problem 3

If z=exy, where x=tcost and y=sint, find dzdt at t=0.

Reveal solution

The chain rule gives dzdt=exy(yx′+xy′). At zero, x=y=0, so the derivative is 0.

Problem 4

Find the directional derivative of f(x,y)=ln(x2+y2) at (1,1) toward (4,5).

Reveal solution

The unit direction is u=⟨3/5,4/5⟩, and ∇f(1,1)=⟨1,1⟩. Thus Duf=75.
