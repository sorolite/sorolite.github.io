Problem 1

Use the chain rule to find dz/dt\operatorname{d}z/\operatorname{d}t if z=x2+y2z=x^2+y^2, x=tx=t, y=t2y=t^2.

Reveal solution

By the chain rule, dz/dt=(∂z/∂x)(dx/dt)+(∂z/∂y)(dy/dt)\operatorname{d}z/\operatorname{d}t = \left( \partial z / \partial x \right) \left( \operatorname{d} x / \operatorname{d} t \right) + \left( \partial z / \partial y \right) \left( \operatorname{d} y / \operatorname{d} t \right).

The partial derivatives of zz are ∂z/∂x=2x\partial z / \partial x = 2x and ∂z/∂y=2y\partial z / \partial y = 2y. The derivatives of xx and yy with respect to tt are, respectively, dx/dt=1\operatorname{d} x / \operatorname{d} t = 1 and dy/dt=2t\operatorname{d} y / \operatorname{d} t = 2t.

Therefore dzdt=∂z∂xdxdt+∂z∂ydydt=(2x)(1)+(2y)(2t)=2x+2ty=2t+2t(t2)=2t+2t3 \begin{align*} \frac{\operatorname{d} z}{\operatorname{d} t} & = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \frac{\operatorname{d} x}{\operatorname{d} t} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \frac{\operatorname{d} y}{\operatorname{d} t} \\ & = (2x)(1) + (2y)(2t) \\ & = 2x + 2ty \\ & = 2t + 2t(t^2) \\ & = 2t + 2t^3 \end{align*}
