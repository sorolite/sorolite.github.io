Optimization

Critical points and constraints in several variables.

Problem 1

Classify the critical point of f(x,y)=x2+4xy+y2.

Reveal solution

The gradient vanishes only at the origin. The discriminant is D=fxxfyy−fxy2=4−16=−12. Since D<0, the origin is a saddle point.

Problem 2

Find the maximum and minimum of f=x+y on x2+y2=1.

Reveal solution

Lagrange multipliers give ⟨1,1⟩=λ⟨2x,2y⟩, so x=y. The constraint gives x=y=±1/2. Thus the maximum is 2 and the minimum is −2.

Problem 3

Find the point on the plane x+y+z=6 closest to the origin.

Reveal solution

Minimize x2+y2+z2. The multiplier equations imply x=y=z. The constraint then gives (x,y,z)=(2,2,2).

Problem 4

Find the absolute extrema of f=xy on the closed disk x2+y2≤4.

Reveal solution

The interior critical point gives value 0. On the boundary, set x=2cosθ and y=2sinθ, so xy=2sin(2θ). The maximum is 2 at (2,2) and its negative counterpart; the minimum is −2 at the two points with opposite-signed coordinates of magnitude 2.
