Problem 1

Let 𝐮=⟨−8,−2,11⟩\mathbf{u} = \left\langle -8, -2, 11 \right\rangle and 𝐯=⟨−6,8,24⟩\mathbf{v} = \left\langle -6, 8, 24 \right\rangle. Find −2𝐮−2𝐯-2\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v}.

Reveal solution

We add vectors component-by-component.

The first component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is −8-8 and the first component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is −6-6. Then −2(−8)−2(−6)=28-2(-8)-2(-6)=28, therefore the first component of −2𝐮−2𝐯-2\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v} is 2828.

The second component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is −2-2 and the second component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is 88. Then −2(−2)−2(8)=−12-2(-2)-2(8)=-12, therefore the second component of −2𝐮−2𝐯-2\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v} is −12-12.

The third component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is 1111 and the third component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is 2424. Then −2(11)−2(24)=−70-2(11)-2(24)=-70, therefore the third component of −2𝐮−2𝐯-2\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v} is −70-70.

Therefore −2𝐮−2𝐯=⟨28,−12,−70⟩-2\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v}=\left\langle 28, -12, -70 \right\rangle.

Problem 2

Let 𝐮=⟨−19,−3,−28⟩\mathbf{u} = \left\langle -19, -3, -28 \right\rangle and 𝐯=−22𝐢−17𝐣−29𝐤\mathbf{v} = -22\mathbf{i} -17\mathbf{j} -29\mathbf{k}. Find −5𝐮−5𝐯-5\mathbf{u}-5\mathbf{v}.

Reveal solution

The first component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is −19-19 and the first component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is −22-22. Then −5(−19)−5(−22)=205-5(-19)-5(-22)=205, therefore the first component of −5𝐮−5𝐯-5\mathbf{u}-5\mathbf{v} is 2828.

The second component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is −3-3 and the second component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is −17-17. Then −5(−17)−5(−3)=100-5(-17)-5(-3)=100, therefore the second component of −5𝐮−5𝐯-5\mathbf{u}-5\mathbf{v} is 100100.

The third component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is −28-28 and the third component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is −29-29. Then −5(−28)−5(−29)=235-5(-28)-5(-29)=235, therefore the third component of −5𝐮−5𝐯-5\mathbf{u}-5\mathbf{v} is 235235.

Therefore −5𝐮−5𝐯=⟨28,100,235⟩-5\mathbf{u}-5\mathbf{v}=\left\langle 28, 100, 235 \right\rangle.

Problem 3

Let 𝐮=15𝐢−24𝐣+14𝐤\mathbf{u} = 15\mathbf{i} -24\mathbf{j} +14\mathbf{k} and 𝐯=⟨−14,−23,0⟩\mathbf{v} = \left\langle -14, -23, 0 \right\rangle. Find 𝐮−6𝐯\mathbf{u}-6\mathbf{v}.

Reveal solution

The first component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is 1515 and the first component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is −14-14. Then 15−6(−14)=9915-6(-14)=99, therefore the first component of 𝐮−6𝐯\mathbf{u}-6\mathbf{v} is 9999.

The second component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is −24-24 and the second component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is −23-23. Then −24−6(−23)=114-24-6(-23)=114, therefore the second component of 𝐮−6𝐯\mathbf{u}-6\mathbf{v} is 114114.

The third component of 𝐮\mathbf{u} is 1414 and the third component of 𝐯\mathbf{v} is 00. Then 14−6(0)=1414-6(0)=14, therefore the third component of 𝐮−6𝐯\mathbf{u}-6\mathbf{v} is 1414.

Therefore 𝐮−6𝐯=⟨99,114,14⟩\mathbf{u}-6\mathbf{v}=\left\langle 99, 114, 14 \right\rangle.

Problem 4

Let 𝐮=27𝐢−15𝐣−8𝐤\mathbf{u} = 27\mathbf{i} -15\mathbf{j} -8\mathbf{k} and 𝐯=5𝐢+19𝐣+25𝐤\mathbf{v} = 5\mathbf{i} +19\mathbf{j} +25\mathbf{k}. Find −2𝐮+2𝐯-2\mathbf{u} +2\mathbf{v}.

Reveal solution

−2⟨27,−15−8⟩+2⟨5,19,25⟩=⟨−2(27)+2(5),−2(−15)+2(19),−2(−8)+2(25)⟩=⟨−44,68,66⟩. \begin{align*} -2\left\langle 27, -15 -8 \right\rangle +2 \left\langle 5, 19, 25 \right\rangle & = \left\langle -2(27)+2(5), -2(-15)+2(19), -2(-8)+2(25) \right\rangle \\ & = \left\langle -44, 68, 66 \right\rangle. \end{align*}

Problem 5

Let 𝐮=⟨−30,19,−24⟩\mathbf{u} = \left\langle -30, 19, -24 \right\rangle and 𝐯=⟨−17,−13,−3⟩\mathbf{v} = \left\langle -17, -13, -3 \right\rangle. Find 𝐮−3𝐯\mathbf{u}-3\mathbf{v}.

Reveal solution

⟨−30,19,−24⟩−3⟨−17,−13,−3⟩=⟨−30−3(−17),19−3(−13),−24−3(−3)⟩=⟨21,58,−15⟩. \begin{align*} \left\langle -30, 19, -24 \right\rangle -3 \left\langle -17, -13, -3 \right\rangle & = \left\langle -30-3(-17), 19-3(-13), -24-3(-3) \right\rangle \\ & = \left\langle 21, 58, -15 \right\rangle. \end{align*}

Problem 6

Let 𝐮=⟨−17,−22,16⟩\mathbf{u} = \left\langle -17, -22, 16 \right\rangle and 𝐯=−18𝐢−19𝐣+8𝐤\mathbf{v} = -18\mathbf{i} -19\mathbf{j} +8\mathbf{k}. Find −4𝐮+3𝐯-4\mathbf{u}+3\mathbf{v}.

−4⟨−17,−22,16⟩+3⟨−18,−19,8⟩=⟨−4(−17)+3(−18),−4(−22)+3(−19),−4(16)+3(8)⟩=⟨14,31,−40⟩. \begin{align*} -4\left\langle -17, -22, 16 \right\rangle +3 \left\langle -18, -19, 8 \right\rangle & = \left\langle -4(-17)+3(-18), -4(-22)+3(-19), -4(16)+3(8) \right\rangle \\ & = \left\langle 14, 31, -40 \right\rangle. \end{align*}

Problem 7

Let 𝐮=−3𝐢+20𝐣−30𝐤\mathbf{u} = -3\mathbf{i} +20\mathbf{j} -30\mathbf{k} and 𝐯=⟨10,−5,2⟩\mathbf{v} = \left\langle 10, -5, 2 \right\rangle. Find 𝐮+2𝐯\mathbf{u}+2\mathbf{v}.
